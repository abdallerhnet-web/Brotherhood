HOW TO PLAY

👆Tap to jump. Tap again in the air for one double jump per jump.
🐻Bears: jump over them. A hit costs you a wolf.
❗Red ! means an arrow is about to fly in. Stay low, or jump clear above it. Never jump into it.
🛡️Shield: blocks exactly one hit.
🏠The gate only opens when all six wolves are with you. Lost wolves return as pickups.
🎵Every wolf is an instrument. Alpha: kick. Silver: hi-hats. Onyx: bass. Scout: arpeggio. Vanguard: snare. Anchor: lead melody. Lose a wolf, lose its sound.
🔥Pack Streak: dodge, rescue wolves, grab shields and land double jumps to build it. Losing a wolf breaks it.
✨Golden wolves and shields are rare. High pickups are risky but worth more.
